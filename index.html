League office

Rules & regulations

Fair play. Competitive integrity. Respect.

Competition basics

The EPL is designed for European players competing in Roblox Practical Basketball. The league office should publish the official season format before fixtures begin.

  • Teams must register an approved roster.
  • Players must use their registered Roblox account.
  • Match results must be submitted with evidence if disputed.
  • Only authorised officials may confirm final scores.

Conduct & disputes

  • Respect players, captains, officials and staff.
  • Harassment, cheating and match manipulation may result in discipline.
  • Captains must submit protests within the time limit set by league staff.
  • Admins should record decisions and apply rules consistently.
These are starter rules, not a final rulebook. League owners should customise and approve them before launch.
